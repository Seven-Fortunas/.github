# Security Policy

This policy covers every Seven Fortunas repository on GitHub that doesn't have its own. [7fchain](https://github.com/Seven-Fortunas/7fchain/blob/main/SECURITY.md) has its own policy, which lists what's in scope for the chain.

## Report a vulnerability

Report it privately, never in a public issue or discussion:

- **GitHub:** **Security → Report a vulnerability** on the affected repository.
- **Email:** security@sevenfortunas.com

Include what's affected, the steps to reproduce it, what an attacker could do with it, and how to reach you. We'll confirm we received it within three business days, keep you updated while we fix it, and agree a disclosure date with you.

## Safe harbor

We treat good-faith research under this policy as authorized, and we won't take legal action against you for it. Good faith means you:

- test only with your own accounts, keys and data, and stop as soon as you reach anything that isn't yours;
- don't degrade our services, the network or anyone else's systems;
- keep the details private until the agreed disclosure date.

Social engineering, phishing and physical attacks against our people, miners or users are never authorized.

## We will never ask for your secrets

Nobody from Seven Fortunas will ever ask for your recovery phrase, passwords, API key or key files, by email, in Discussions or anywhere else. Anyone who does is not us: report it to security@sevenfortunas.com. We contact you only from @sevenfortunas.com addresses or from the [Seven-Fortunas](https://github.com/Seven-Fortunas) organization on GitHub.
