# Security Policy

This policy covers every Seven Fortunas repository on GitHub that doesn't have its own. [7fchain](https://github.com/Seven-Fortunas/7fchain/blob/main/SECURITY.md) has its own policy, which lists what's in scope for the chain.

## Report a vulnerability

**Don't open a public issue or discussion for a security problem.** Report it privately instead:

- **Email:** security@sevenfortunas.com
- **GitHub:** where a repository offers it, use **Security → Report a vulnerability** on that repository.

Please include what's affected, the steps to reproduce it, what an attacker could do with it, and how to reach you.

We'll confirm we received your report, keep you updated while we work on it, and agree a disclosure date with you. Please give us a reasonable time to ship a fix before you publish details.

## Please don't

- access, change or delete data that isn't yours;
- disrupt our services or other people's systems;
- use social engineering or physical attacks against Seven Fortunas staff or users.
